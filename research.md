      <div class="research-area-copy">
        <p class="research-narrative">An AI for healthcare direction that translates continuous daily observations into clinically meaningful trajectories connecting hospital, rehabilitation and home. <a href="https://mhealth.jmir.org/2019/7/e14090/"><em>JMIR mHealth and uHealth</em>, 2019</a>: We built the sensing layer by combining smartwatch-based activity recognition with indoor localization to quantify mobility and behavior during rehabilitation. <a href="https://mhealth.jmir.org/2022/5/e23887/"><em>JMIR mHealth and uHealth</em>, 2022</a>: We extended this sensing framework into longitudinal modeling to reveal how daily activity and recovery evolve over time. <a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/digital-health/articles/10.3389/fdgth.2024.1399461/full"><em>Frontiers in Digital Health</em>, 2024</a>: We completed the pipeline with scalable, HIPAA-compliant cloud infrastructure for the secure collection and processing of continuous remote-monitoring data.</p>
